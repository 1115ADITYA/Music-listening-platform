## Best Ways to Watch YouTube with Friends

There are a few different ways to **watch YouTube with friends online**, and each has its pros and cons.

### 1. Screen sharing on Discord, Zoom, or Meet

This is the method most people try first.

**Pros:**

* Easy if you're already on a call
* No separate watch room

**Cons:**

* Video quality depends on the host's internet
* Can introduce noticeable delay
* Higher bandwidth usage
* Playback isn't actually synced for everyone

---

### 2. Browser extensions

Some browser extensions can sync YouTube playback between friends.

**Pros:**

* Better synchronization than screen sharing

**Cons:**

* Everyone usually needs to install the same extension
* Often limited to desktop browsers
* Can break when YouTube updates

This is usually the easiest option.

Instead of streaming your screen, everyone watches directly from YouTube while a shared room keeps playback synchronized.
